The real problem

Your lineup is locked. Your people still need somewhere to sleep.

Hotel blocks fill up fast, sit far from site, and don't flex when your crew count changes two weeks out. Standard tent rentals mean poles, stakes, and a setup crew you don't have time for during load-in. You need accommodation that goes up fast, lives on-site, and scales with whoever actually needs a bed that weekend — artists, crew, volunteers, or VIPs.

Meet the K1 Summit Shelter

One shelter. No poles. Set up by your own crew.

The K1 Summit Shelter is a large, fully inflatable, pole-free camping shelter — about 160 square feet, roughly 150" × 142" × 87", and around 75 lbs. It inflates with the included pump instead of assembling a pole frame, which means your production crew can set up accommodation without hiring a separate setup team.

Because each shelter is a standalone unit, you rent exactly as many as your event needs — and we help you figure out the right number for artist, staff, and VIP accommodation based on your actual headcount.
