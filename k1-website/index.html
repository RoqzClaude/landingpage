Questions Before You Book

Will the tent fit in my car?

Yes — the whole setup packs down into a couple of duffel-sized bags that fit easily in the trunk of a car or SUV. Here's what pickup actually looks like, packed into a Ford Escape — which can fit 2 tents in the back:

How long does setup take?

Less than a minute with an electric pump, or 2–3 minutes with the hand pump we provide — no poles to assemble, no instructions to figure out.

What are the dimensions of the tent?

150" L x 142" W x 87" H — about 160 square feet.

How many people will it sleep?

5–6 comfortably with proper beds — more if sleeping on the ground.

Do I need to pick up and drop off the tent myself?

Yes — pickup and drop-off are both at our NW Edmonton location, 12331 St Albert Trail NW.
