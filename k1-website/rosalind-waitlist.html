Questions About The Tent

How durable are they?

Very — these are heavy-duty inflatable AirPole tents, not a backyard pop-up. They're built to hold up to repeated festival use and stay inflated for months once set up properly.

Are they weatherproof?

Yes — the fabric and rain fly are built to handle rain. You'll stay dry and comfortable no matter what the weekend forecast looks like.

What happens if it's windy?

The tents are properly staked and anchored on setup to handle normal festival-weekend wind. Our team sets everything up, so you don't need to worry about securing it yourself.

What do I need to bring?

Just yourself, your group, and whatever you'd normally pack for a weekend at the festival. The tent, beds, bedding, and lights are already set up and waiting for you — no gear to haul or set up yourself.

Is there a damage deposit?

Yes — a damage deposit is collected ahead of time and fully returned after a quick inspection once the festival wraps up.
