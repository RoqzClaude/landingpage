What We Rent, And Who It's For

Four ways people use K1 — pick the one that fits your trip.

For individuals & families

Glamping Getaways

A fully furnished tent, ready to pick up from our NW Edmonton location and take to your own site or a campground. No gear to buy, no setup to figure out — just a comfortable place to sleep under the stars.

Good for a weekend trip, a first camping experience, or anyone who'd rather skip the tent-pole wrestling match.

For weddings

Overnight Glamping For Your Wedding

An overnight option for guests and the wedding party, set up right at the venue. No one has to drive home after the reception, and no one has to book a hotel block.

We work with venues to find a layout that fits the property and the day-of schedule.

We offer additional delivery service for wedding bookings — standard orders are pickup from our NW Edmonton location, with delivery and on-site setup available on request.

For festivals & events

Festival & Event Camping

Tents for festival campgrounds, VIP areas, and anywhere an event needs on-site accommodation. We work with organizers to size the order to the event — one tent or a whole field of them.

Standard orders are pickup from our NW Edmonton location — delivery and on-site setup can be arranged for larger event orders, just ask.
