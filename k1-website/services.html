For individuals & families

Glamping Getaways

A fully furnished tent, ready to pick up from our NW Edmonton location and take to your own site or a campground. No gear to buy, no setup to figure out — just a comfortable place to sleep under the stars.

Good for a weekend trip, a first camping experience, or anyone who'd rather skip the tent-pole wrestling match.
