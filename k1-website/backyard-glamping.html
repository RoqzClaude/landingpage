FAQ

Questions Before You Book

How much space do I need?

The tent is 150" x 142" (about 160 square feet) — leave a bit of extra room around it for the rain fly and guy lines. See the tent for full specs.

How long does setup take?

Less than a minute with an electric pump, or 2–3 minutes with the hand pump we provide — no poles to assemble.

Do I need to pick up and drop off the tent myself?

Yes — pickup and drop-off are both at our NW Edmonton location, 12331 St Albert Trail NW.

How many people will it sleep?

5–6 comfortably with proper beds — plenty of room for a sleepover crew.
