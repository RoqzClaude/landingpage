Why I Started K1 Outdoors

DRAFT — to be rewritten from James's notes

I started K1 Outdoors because I kept noticing the same thing: people wanted to get outside, but the gear and the setup kept getting in the way. A trip that should've been about the lake, the mountains, or just a weekend off instead turned into wrestling a tent in the dark or spending a paycheque on gear you'd use twice a year.

K1 is my answer to that. We deliver a tent that's already set up, already comfortable, and already clean — so the only thing left to do is show up. That's the whole idea behind "Camping Made Easy," and it's the promise I hold every rental to.

— James, Edmonton, AB
