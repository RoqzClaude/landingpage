FAQ

Running An Event?

Can you handle large group orders?

Yes — we work with organizers to size the order to the event, from a handful of tents to a full village. Contact us with your numbers and dates.

Do you deliver to festival sites?

Standard orders are pickup from our NW Edmonton location — delivery and on-site setup can be arranged for larger event orders, just ask.

How far in advance should we book?

As early as possible, especially for festival season — reach out as soon as your event dates are set.

What's included with each tent?

The tent, mallet, pegs, rain fly, hand pump, room divider, and a repair kit, with a choice of air mattress or cots. See pricing for add-ons.
