Why Would You Choose An Inflatable Tent For A Wedding?

Traditionally, overnight wedding guests are stuck with two bad options: drive off property to a hotel after the reception, or squeeze into a cramped, uncomfortable tent that takes away from the experience. A K1 glamping tent fixes both — guests stay right at the venue, in a real bed, with room to actually be comfortable.

FAQ

Planning A Wedding?

Do you deliver and set up for weddings?

Standard orders are pickup from our NW Edmonton location, but delivery and on-site setup are available on request for wedding bookings — just ask when you reach out.

How many tents can you provide?

Contact us with your guest count and we'll work out how many tents and what layout fits your venue.

How far in advance should we book?

The sooner the better, especially for peak wedding season — check available dates as soon as you have a venue confirmed.

What's included with each tent?

The tent, mallet, pegs, rain fly, hand pump, room divider, and a repair kit, with a choice of air mattress or cots. See pricing for add-ons like lighting and electric pump setup.
